# Phase 29.5 Plan 02: ci/main verified diff tool Summary

The jq-only `29.5-ci-main-verified-diff.sh` compares two ci/main lifecycle-assert snapshots. `--mode fix` returns FAIL only on a regression: a new triage-open Trivy finding with verified=true, a non-Trivy verified change, or an open Trivy finding that went from false to true. It reports `still_true` and `reset_needed` as measurements. `--mode reset` returns FAIL on an incomplete reset or on any change outside the reset's scope. On the real Phase 29.4 snapshot, `fix B vs B` gives PASS with `before_open_trivy 65` (5 trivy-fs + 60 trivy-image), `still_true 65` and `reset_needed true`. That matches the counts measured in the plan.
