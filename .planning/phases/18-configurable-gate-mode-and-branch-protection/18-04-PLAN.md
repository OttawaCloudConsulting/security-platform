<notes>
**Set no repository variable in this plan.** The whole point is that `GATE_MODE` is unset, so the run
exercises the `|| 'report-only'` tail of the chain. Confirm it is unset BEFORE the run rather than
assuming; 18-05 is what sets it, and 18-05 is what removes it again.

**Do not merge.** The PR stays open. 18-05 re-runs on it and 18-08 merges it under human confirmation.

**If a check concludes `failure` in report-only, STOP and report — do not relax anything.** With
`gate_mode` unset, a red scan job means the expression is not evaluating as designed. The intolerant
report-content and upload-verify steps CAN legitimately turn a job red on a real infrastructure failure;
tell the two apart by reading which step failed, and say which one it was.

**Read the mode, do not infer it.** Every job echoes `gate_mode=<value>`. Five occurrences of
`gate_mode=report-only` in the run log is the evidence. A single job showing something else is a finding.

**Do not "fix" a missing sixth `security` check.** Its absence is the expected, evidenced result.
</notes>
