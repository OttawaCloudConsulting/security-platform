## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Accuracy] Pre-merge state wording**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The plan action says that for client-side refusals the ADR should state that "gh read BLOCKED". The
  evidence shows that the script's pre-merge read returned `UNKNOWN` in all six attempts (a3 read
  `UNKNOWN`/`MERGEABLE`). BLOCKED was read only for PR-0 (plan 07).
- **Fix:** The ADR records the measured `UNKNOWN` values. It says BLOCKED is inferred from gh's message for the race
  attempts, and it confines the BLOCKED read to PR-0.
- **Commit:** 0ced1b6

**2. [Rule 1 - Accuracy] Who performed the writes**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The plan action says "every write by the operator". The evidence (29.6-07-refusal.txt,
  29.6-09/10-race-run.txt, 29.6-11-repo-delete.txt) shows the orchestrating session ran the writes on approval. The
  operator ran only two actions: the PR-0 merge attempt and the delete.
- **Fix:** The ADR states the actual actors.
- **Commit:** 0ced1b6

**3. [Rule 2 - Correctness] Added NOT-verified items beyond the plan list**

  Leaving these out would over-claim what the evidence covers.
- **Fix:** NOT-verified items 4, 5 and 8 were added: whether variant (b) hit the window, the sub-second pre-check-run
  window, and the delete method.
- **Commit:** 0ced1b6
