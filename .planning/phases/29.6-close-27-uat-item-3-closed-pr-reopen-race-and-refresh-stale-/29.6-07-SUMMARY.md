## What this proves and what it does not

It proves that once the ruleset was applied, GitHub reported PR-0 as BLOCKED on a red required check, and that gh
declined the merge because of that state. It does not prove that the server's merge endpoint itself rejects a merge
request. D-19 forbids a direct REST merge in this phase, so ADR-019 NOT-verified item 2 stays open. The race may close
it if it produces a server-side refusal.
