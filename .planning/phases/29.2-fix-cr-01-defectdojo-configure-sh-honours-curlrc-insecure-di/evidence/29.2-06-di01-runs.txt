# 29.2-06 DI-01 evidence (2026-09-30, uid 501, macOS, python3 3.12.0, curl 8.7.1 LibreSSL)

## 1. Natural reproduction attempts, before the fix (instrumented, not committed)

A temporary diagnostic in curlrc_counts printed the log whenever CONNECT > HANDSHAKE-FAIL + REQUEST at count time.
30 sequential runs plus 48 runs under 6-way parallel load: 78 runs, all PROOF PASS - 30 assertions.
The late-HANDSHAKE-FAIL race was NOT observed naturally in these 78 runs (dd-import and dd-delete were always settled).
The diagnostic fired in every run for one target, deterministically, and it is not a race:

    RACE-DIAG proof-curlrc-homelab.log before C=6 H=4 R=0 log=[CONNECT|HANDSHAKE-FAIL SSLError|CONNECT|CONNECT|HANDSHAKE-FAIL SSLError|CONNECT|CONNECT|HANDSHAKE-FAIL SSLError|CONNECT|HANDSHAKE-FAIL SSLError|]
    RACE-DIAG after C=6 H=4 R=0   (0.5 s later: unchanged)

Cause: defectdojo-homelab-validate.sh:483 runs 'openssl s_client' (cert dump). It does not abort on a verify failure, so the
server-side handshake completes and the client closes with no request. Those 2 connections never get a HANDSHAKE-FAIL line, so
the orchestrator's predicate CONNECT == HANDSHAKE-FAIL + REQUEST never holds for homelab.
